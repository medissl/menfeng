## Layout

- Use a left-aligned menu column over a soft dark fade. Give the title and settings the same door still. The pause overlay uses the current gameplay frame at its current crop. Keep the right half of the art visible.
- Start around 80–100 px from the left at 1280 × 720. Keep heading and setting labels aligned. Give menu rows at least 44 px of clickable height, with an even rhythm.
- Use a compact upper-right icon and **Menu** label with a plain dark backplate. Keep that location across gameplay, including tape playback. While paused, show **Resume** as the way back and hide the corner control. Escape opens or closes the pause overlay.
- Keep the gameplay reading group at bottom center. Left-align the speaker name to the text inside the centered column. Use a readable serif for narration and dialogue, with the name appearing when it identifies a speaker.

## Controls

| Control | Visual treatment and behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Menu row | At rest, warm ivory over charcoal. On hover or keyboard focus, brighten the label and show a red dash plus a faint charcoal strip. Use a brief stronger wash while pressed. |
| Master volume | Reuse the Settings example in both menus. Use a thin track with a filled segment, a distinct ivory thumb, and a right-aligned percentage. Keep the drag target taller than the visible line. |
| Reduce motion | Show both values, **Off** and **On**. The current value gets a charcoal backing and a red underline. Use the separate row focus mark for navigation so focus and the saved value read independently. |
| Resume / Back / Return to title | Use text rows with full click areas. Give **Resume** first focus on pause. Keep **Return to title** clearly readable and lower in the column. |
| Continue | Center it under the reading group with a small down chevron. It advances the current line. |
| Tape transport | Put **Pause tape** and **Stand up** in a separate lower-right group. Change **Pause tape** to **Resume tape** when the tape is paused, and use **Play tape** or **Replay tape** when applicable. Keep the physical deck hotspot and replace the debug-like outline with the same hover or focus treatment used for scene objects. |

Use VCR OSD Mono-style lettering for **PLAY**, playback state, and the counter inside the CRT. Use readable ivory serif and sans type for the player interface. Keep scanlines and tracking effects on the footage. Match the palette, proportions, and hierarchy with live Godot controls.
